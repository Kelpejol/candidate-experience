## How do I receive my test invitation?
Your invitation is sent to the email address you registered with, and it includes your test link and instructions. Please check your inbox, and also your spam or promotions folder, since it can sometimes land there.

## I did not receive my invitation email
Please check your spam and promotions folders first, and search for an email from Dragnet. If you still can't find it, the team can look into it for you, and I can connect you to a colleague who will help.

## Where do I find my test link?
Your test link is inside your invitation email. Open that email and tap the link, or copy it into your browser to begin.

## How long is my test link valid?
Your link is valid for the assessment window shown in your invitation email, so please check those dates and try to take your test within that period.

## Can I use my link on a different device?
Yes, you can open your link on a supported device, but please only start the test once, on a stable connection, since some assessments cannot be restarted.
