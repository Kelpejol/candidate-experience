## How long does the test take?
The duration is shown in your invitation email and again on the screen before you begin, so please make sure you have enough uninterrupted time before you start.

## Can I pause the test once I start?
Most assessments are timed and can't be paused, so please only begin when you're ready, in a quiet place with a stable connection.

## Can I go back to a previous question?
That depends on the assessment. Some let you move back and others don't. The instructions on your screen will tell you before you begin.

## I accidentally submitted my test
Please don't try to start again. Let me connect you to a colleague who can look into what happened with your submission.

## What happens if I run out of time?
Most timed assessments submit automatically when the time is up, keeping the answers you've completed. If you're worried about your attempt, a colleague can help.

## Can I take a break during the test?
Because the test is timed, stepping away will use up your time. It's best to complete it in one sitting once you begin.
