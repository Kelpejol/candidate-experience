## How can I reschedule my exam?
Rescheduling depends on the assessment window set by the client, and it's confirmed by our team. I can send you a secure link to submit your request and details, and the team will review it for you.

## I missed my test window
Whether a missed assessment can be retaken is decided for each campaign by the client. I can send you a link to submit your details so the team can look into it.

## How much notice do I need to reschedule?
There isn't a fixed rule, since it depends on the client's window, but the sooner you let us know the better. I can send you a link to submit your request.

## Can I change my test date myself?
Test dates are managed by our team rather than changed directly by candidates. I can send you a link to request a change, and the team will confirm.

## I have a clash or emergency on my test date
I'm sorry to hear that. I can send you a link to submit your situation and details, and the team will review whether a change is possible.
