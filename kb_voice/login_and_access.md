## I can't log in to my test
Please make sure you're using the exact link and details from your invitation email, and that you have a stable internet connection. If it still won't let you in, I can connect you to a colleague to check your account.

## I forgot my password
Use the password reset option on the login page if there is one, or check your invitation email for your access details. If you're still stuck, a colleague can help restore your access.

## My username or password is not working
Please re-check the details in your invitation email and make sure there are no extra spaces. If they still don't work, I'll connect you to a colleague who can verify your account.

## My activation link is not working
Sometimes a link expires or has already been used. Please try opening it again from the original email. If it still fails, the team can issue a fresh one, and I can connect you.

## I'm locked out of my account
Let me help get this sorted. I'll connect you to a colleague who can safely check your account and restore your access.
