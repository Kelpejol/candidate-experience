## What browser or device should I use?
For the best experience, use an up to date browser like Chrome or Firefox on a laptop or desktop, with a stable internet connection. It helps to close other tabs and apps before you start.

## The test page won't load
Please check your internet connection, refresh the page, and try a different browser if you can. If the page still won't load, I can connect you to a colleague.

## The test froze or my screen went blank
Try refreshing the page and reconnecting. If the assessment allows it, you may be able to continue where you left off. If it won't recover, let me connect you to a colleague right away.

## I'm having trouble uploading my passport photograph
Please make sure your photo matches the size and format shown on the page, and that your connection is steady. Trying a different browser often helps. If it still won't upload, a colleague can assist.

## Do I need a camera or microphone?
Some assessments are monitored and may ask for your camera. Your invitation email will say if that's required. If you're not sure, I can connect you to a colleague to confirm.

## My internet disconnected during the test
Please reconnect as quickly as you can and reopen your test. Many assessments let you continue from where you stopped. If you can't get back in, let me connect you to a colleague immediately.

## The test is very slow
A slow connection or a busy device is the usual cause. Try closing other tabs and apps, and move closer to your router if you can. If it stays slow, a colleague can look into it with you.
