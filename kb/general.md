# General

> DRAFT CONTENT — wording must be reviewed by the Candidate Experience team.

## Candidate asks about their application status
Application and shortlisting decisions belong to the recruiting client, not
Dragnet. Thank the candidate for their interest and explain that candidates
who progress are contacted directly with next steps. Dragnet cannot share
shortlisting outcomes or timelines beyond what the client has announced.

## Candidate asks general questions about how the assessment works
Candidates should: use an updated Chrome or Edge browser on a computer (not a
phone, unless the invitation says otherwise), sit in a quiet well-lit room for
proctored tests, keep their ID available if the invitation requires identity
checks, complete the practice test first, and start the real test early within
the window rather than at the last minute.

## What we never answer by email (for KB editors)
Test results, score disputes, eligibility decisions, payment or scholarship
disbursement questions, identity or record changes, legal or privacy matters,
and complaints are always handled by a Candidate Experience officer directly.
Do not add KB sections that answer these topics — the AI must escalate them,
never answer them. This section documents the boundary for future editors.
