# Technical Issues

> DRAFT CONTENT — wording must be reviewed by the Candidate Experience team
> before go-live. Every section becomes one retrievable KB chunk.

## Candidate cannot access the practice or demo test
If the practice test page is not displaying or loading: ask the candidate to
use an updated Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge browser, clear the browser
cache, disable browser extensions, and try the test link in an
incognito/private window. If it still fails, they should try a different
device or a different network (mobile hotspot often helps). Reassure them that
the practice test does not affect their actual assessment score.

## Candidate has not received login credentials (username and password)
Ask the candidate to check their spam/junk folder for the credentials email
and confirm the email address they registered with. If nothing is found,
credentials can be re-sent: collect their full name, registered email address,
and the test/campaign name, and escalate to the Candidate Experience team for
a resend. Do not guess or invent credentials.

## Camera or microphone not detected during a proctored test
Ask the candidate to: allow camera and microphone permissions for the browser
when prompted; close every other application that may be using the camera
(Zoom, Teams, WhatsApp Desktop); use Chrome or Edge; and restart the browser
after changing permissions. On Windows, check Settings > Privacy > Camera to
confirm browser access is enabled. If the device has no working camera, the
candidate should switch to a device that does before the test window closes.

## Talview Secure Browser will not install or log in
The candidate should download the Talview Secure Browser only from the link
in their invitation email, temporarily disable antivirus/firewall blocking if
installation fails, and log in with the exact credentials from the invitation.
Company/office laptops often block installation — advise using a personal
computer. If installation remains impossible, escalate for assistance before
the test window closes.

## Test was interrupted or candidate was locked out mid-assessment
Apologise for the disruption and reassure the candidate that interruptions
are logged. Collect: full name, registered email, test/campaign name, what
happened, and any error message shown. Escalate to the Candidate Experience
team, who can confirm whether the attempt can be resumed or reset. Do not
promise a reset — only officers can confirm one.

## Upload or submission error during application or test
Ask the candidate to confirm the file meets the stated format and size limits,
try a different browser, and retry on a stable connection. If the deadline is
close and uploads still fail, collect their details and the exact error
message and escalate so the team can log the attempt before the deadline.
