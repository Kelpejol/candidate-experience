# Test Invitations

> DRAFT CONTENT — wording must be reviewed by the Candidate Experience team.

## Candidate confirms receipt or availability for a test
No reply is normally required when a candidate simply confirms receipt of a
test invitation or their availability. If a response is expected, thank them,
confirm their slot is noted, and remind them to complete the practice test
beforehand and to sit the assessment in a quiet place with a stable internet
connection.

## Candidate did not receive a test invitation they expected
Ask the candidate to check spam/junk folders and to search their inbox for
the campaign name. Confirm which email address they applied with. If the
invitation is genuinely missing, collect their full name, registered email,
and the campaign/test name, and escalate to the Candidate Experience team to
verify their eligibility and resend the invitation.

## Invitation email bounced or was undeliverable
If a candidate reports (or a bounce notification shows) that their invitation
could not be delivered: confirm the correct email address with the candidate,
collect the corrected address together with their full name and campaign, and
escalate so the invitation can be re-sent to the corrected address.

## Candidate asks when their test will take place or the deadline
The test date, time window, and deadline are stated in the invitation email.
Direct the candidate to the invitation for the authoritative schedule. If the
candidate says the invitation contains no date or the window has passed,
escalate to the Candidate Experience team rather than guessing a date.
