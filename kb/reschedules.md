# Rescheduling

> DRAFT CONTENT — wording must be reviewed by the Candidate Experience team.
> The actual reschedule policy is client-specific; officers approve every
> reschedule. The KB only covers how to acknowledge and what to collect.

## Candidate requests a reschedule (illness, emergency, or clash)
Acknowledge the request with empathy. Explain that reschedules depend on the
client's assessment window and are confirmed by the Candidate Experience
team. Collect: full name, registered email, test/campaign name, the scheduled
date they cannot make, and the reason (a medical report or evidence can help
where applicable). Do not confirm or promise a new date — only officers can
approve a reschedule.

## Candidate missed their test window entirely
Empathise, and explain that whether a missed assessment can be retaken is
decided per campaign by the client. Collect their full name, registered
email, campaign name, and reason for missing the window, and escalate to the
Candidate Experience team. Do not state that a retake is or is not possible.
